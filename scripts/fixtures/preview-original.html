Water evidence / 06

Every litre
has a state.

Treatment is not the same thing as independent verification. The interface should make that difference impossible to miss.

  1. 01

    Raw condensate

    Water collected from the condensation pathway before treatment claims are applied.

  2. 02

    Treated water

    Water that has completed the configured treatment pathway. Treatment alone does not certify intended use.

  3. 03

    Verification

    Sampling, laboratory review or another defined verification process is pending or in progress.

  4. 04

    Verified for intended use

    A specific verification result supports a specific stated use. The scope of that verification must remain visible.

Hard questions / 09

No hidden
conditions.

01Can I drink the water?+

Not by default. Condensate can pass through treatment, but drinking-water suitability depends on the configured pathway, local requirements, sampling and verification for the intended use.

02How much can it produce?+

There is no single honest number for every site. Production depends on temperature, relative humidity, system configuration, airflow, surface performance and operating strategy.

03What happens when humidity falls?+

The dew point falls and condensation potential usually decreases. Operation may become less useful.

04How much energy does it use?+

Energy intensity must be measured for the actual system and conditions. This site intentionally does not publish a validated kWh/L figure without supporting data.

05Is the device finished?+

The hardware imagery on this site is concept visualization, not proof of a finalized production device.

06What does a pilot prove?+

A pilot can measure local climate behavior, production, energy intensity, treatment performance, maintenance demands and evidence gaps.
